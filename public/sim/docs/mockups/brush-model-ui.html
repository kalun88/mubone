mubone interface directions · aug 2026 · nothing built

One hand paints, the other commits — at the same time

No rooms, no modes. Numbers are tiles — hold 1, 2 or 3 and move over the sphere; 4, 5, 6 scrape; 7, 8 undo and redo. Q, W, E are the layers. Hold them together: 1 + Q records a line and loops it as it goes. Hold 2 sweeping a circle and tap E as you go to drop clouds mid-gesture. Drag tiles to reorder; the number follows the position.

Layers
One screen

Tiles below, their settings under them, layers beside

Photoshop's layout, with the options bar moved under the tiles so the thing you are adjusting sits next to the thing you pressed. Everything you touch while playing is in one row along the bottom: brushes, erasers, undo, redo, and a + to make more. Numbers run left to right, so the tile's position is its key — nothing to memorise past "first tile is 1".

Numbers rather than letters on purpose: a letter row is a keyboard-layout accident, a number row isn't. Drag a tile and its number follows — the way forScore lets you arrange the tools you actually reach for.

The right bar belongs to layers, permanently — Q, W, E. No arrange room, no mode: the arrangement is always visible, the way a layers panel always is.

The two hands are the point. Numbers and letters sit on different rows so they can be held together, and holding while painting is not a special case — it's the main way to play. 1 + Q is a line recorded and looped in one motion, the way a looper pedal works. Hold 2 through a long sweeping gesture and tap E three times as you go: three clouds dropped into layer 3, mid-stroke, without stopping.

That is what ⇧ would have cost. A modifier makes the second gesture a variation on the first; two separate rows make them independent, and independence is what lets you record and commit at once.

There is no sample bank any more. A stamp tile is a loaded sample — make another and point it at another file. Same for brushes and erasers. That's the whole replacement for twenty presets and ten sample slots: make a tile, keep a tile.

  • Hold 1 + Q — line, looped as you draw
  • Hold 2, tap E a few times mid-sweep
  • Hold 2 then W — a drawn cloud
  • 4 to scrape, 7 to undo
  • Drag a tile to reorder
  • Click a layer's dot to mute, S to solo
  • Raise layer fade, then move
  • Hold 7 and sweep — layers flip
What the brush owns Both halves of a mark — how it goes down and how it reads back forever. That's why there's no mode: a line loops when you touch it because it's a line, not because a switch says loop.

Direction four

A set you can name, reopen and survive a crash with

The concept is already finished in the code — two file types, disjoint on purpose, a version gate, a payload normaliser. What is missing is that the Electron preload exposes 22 IPC methods and not one of them touches the filesystem, so saving is a browser download into ~/Downloads with a timestamp for a name.

Open a recent set from the menu and watch the sphere load different material. That is the whole feature: a thing with a name that you can come back to.

  • Open the Set menu
  • Open a recent set
  • Trigger the recovery dialog
  • Compare with Rig
Trade-off Do this after the material model, not before. A set today is a snapshot of memory; a set under the brush model is a list of strokes — smaller, diffable, and the version worth designing the container around. Building files first means writing the format twice.
Starting point

Why trace is the wrong word, with receipts

You are right to be annoyed by it, and the reason is stronger than taste: the codebase already disagrees with the UI.

js/paint-ticker.js — "Drops a particle at the current cursor position every N milliseconds while painting."
paintGateThreshold · SAMPLE_PAINT_COLORS · LIVE_PAINT_COLORS · updatePaintIndicator() · actions paint1–paint10
index.html, the first-run hint the app shows a new user: "click and drag on the canvas to paint sound."

The app tells new users it paints and then labels the button "trace". Trace is the outlier, not the incumbent — it appears in traceMode, one button, and a doc, and it collides twice: with ui-trace.js (a dead module sharing only the word) and with its own three sub-modes, where "trace" means both the verb and one of the three things the verb can be.

It is also weak as a verb. You trace something that already exists — a shape, an outline. What you actually do here is put new sound into a place. Every candidate below is a stronger verb than that.

Four systems

Pick a metaphor and commit to it all the way down

The failure mode is not a bad word — it is three metaphors at once, which is what the app has now (a drawing word, a version-control word, a gardening word). Each system below is internally consistent. Flip the switch at the top of the page to wear one for a while.

Concept Paint · recommended Voice Tape Today
Paint

Matches the brush model exactly, and every word already appears somewhere in the code. Nothing has to be taught. Risk: it is a visual metaphor for an audible instrument, and "paint" as a mass noun ("there's paint here") takes a beat to land.

Voice

Honest to what mubone is for — a performer sounding into a room. Conduct is the best single word in any of these systems. Risk: hush and earshot are charming in a design doc and precious at a soundcheck.

Tape

Anyone who has opened a DAW is fluent on sight. Zero teaching cost, zero personality — and it quietly reframes the instrument as a recorder, which is the one thing you have consistently said it is not.

The shorter list

The best renames are deletions

Counting what a player currently has to hold in their head — every noun and verb the UI, keyboard doc and OSC namespace expose — comes to about forty terms. Most of them do not need better names. They need to stop existing.

Today's words Becomes Why
seed · seq · cloud · loop · playhead · commit · plant · drop · draw · uproot · liftholdEleven words for one idea: material that keeps sounding after the cursor leaves. What kind it is follows from the brush, so it never needed a name.
scan · scan on/off · triggers on/offdeletedThe cursor always plays what it reaches. The only state left to name is silence, and that word is already mute.
trace mode · commit mode · composer modedeletedThree mode-cycles that all answer "what is the cursor for". The tool answers it once.
patch · preset · user patch · factory patchbrushA saved parameter set is a brush. One concept where there were two, and it is the concept you asked for.
recency · recency depthlayersIt limits how many of the newest buffers under the cursor are audible. That is a layer stack, and every painter already knows what one is.
search radius · trigger radius · erase radiusreachAlready unified in code (#185) — one cursor, one reach. The name just has not caught up. "Radius" describes the geometry; "reach" describes the gesture.
triggerhit"Trigger" is a mechanism; "hit" is a musical event. One syllable, obvious verb, and a percussionist already owns the word.
sweep · erase all · undo strokeerase + clearSweep is erase with a filter; folding it in leaves two destructive words instead of three, and undo is undo.
session export · setup export · importset · rigThe audit already wrote the sentence: setup is the rig, session is the music. A musician calls the music "a set" and the machine "the rig". Export and import become Save and Open.
morph · radial morph · staging · snapshot · pinblendFive names for interpolating between saved parameter sets. Under brushes there is one verb: you blend between brushes, however the blend is being steered.
The sentence to test it against

A player should be able to describe a whole set in one breath, using only words they were never taught:

"I painted a wash across the top, held it, painted three hits low and left, then arranged them out one at a time until only the wash was left."

Fourteen terms, no manual. The same sentence today needs trace, trace+cloud, commit, cloud, drop, seed slot, trigger, arm, composer mode, latch — and a person who already knows the app.

How to land it

Rename in one commit, not gradually

This is the one place where the project's own rule helps: no compat hedging, no legacy aliases. A half-renamed vocabulary is worse than either, because both words are then correct and neither is trusted.

The one to do first and alone is commit → hold, because it is already carrying eleven synonyms and it is the word you already like. Do it as its own pass, delete seedSlots, seqSlots and the rest of the legacy getters in state.js at the same time, and the diff tells you honestly how much of the old model is still load-bearing.

  • Flip the switch to Voice and reread the mockups
  • Then Today, to feel the difference
Watch out for state.js still carries getter/setter aliases mapping seedSlots, seqSlots, seedLockEnabled and friends onto the commit pool. Renaming on top of those means three names for one field. Delete them in the same commit — the project's own note on AXIS_SOURCES says why: delete the name, break loudly.
