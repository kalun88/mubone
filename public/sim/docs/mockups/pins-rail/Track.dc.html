in / out are the pin's ramps — pin and unmute ride in, unpin and mute ride out. A cloud with 10 s on both swells and dies slowly every time; a loop with 0 / 1.5 s cuts in and tails out. Today these are one global pair snapshotted at pin time; here every pin keeps and edits its own.
